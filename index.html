Hidden Stories

Every team draws one secret Bible story.
Find every other team’s story with yes-or-no questions,
and keep yours hidden.

Before round 3
Make up your question

Talk with your team and decide your question now.

  • No names of people or places.
  • No Bible book names or chapter/verse numbers. No “Old Testament” or “New Testament.”
  • No “Is it the story where…?” Ask about what happens or what someone says.
  • The pastor can say “Too direct. Ask another one.”

Tip: ask about a person, an action, or an object you noticed while reading.
